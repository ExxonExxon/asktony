About Us

Founded by Anthony, AskTony is a growing maintenance business focused on domestic and commercial odd issues.

With his 14 years of experience in the trade industry Anthony has been able to acquire several skills. He started working with a master tiler back in Italy while he was studying Electrical trade, once graduated he worked as an electrician then after a few years he moved to Australia. Working as Fencer and home/office renovations then back to tiling, bathroom renovations and general maintenance, he built both the knowledge and the network that enabled him to create AskTony and the team that stands with it.

None of this would be possible without the help of our expertise, Corey, Joe, James and Freddy.

Given his passion about technology, Anthony with AskTony proudly partnered up with the young startup Feex helping the team and its founder on a mission to change the world of tradies and homeowners.
